# Design System: Al Quba Investment

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Transparent Institution"**

Al Quba invests through businesses it actually owns, and the design system exists to make that verifiable rather than merely claimed. Every surface is built to be checked: structure, terms, risks, named subsidiaries, and photographs of the real cargo, cranes, and sites behind each division. Where a lesser system would reach for an abstract gradient or a stock handshake, this one shows the mechanism. Nothing is hidden, so nothing needs decoration to compensate.

The palette is the Gulf at two times of day. **Deep Gulf Navy** carries authority in hero, footer, and closing sections; **Sandstone** — a warmed off-white, never stark — holds the reading surfaces between them. **Desert Gold** is the signature, and it is rationed: a hairline rule under an eyebrow, a stroke beneath a statistic, an active state in navigation, a single primary CTA. It is a ruling instrument, not a fill. Manrope carries the display voice — a geometric grotesque set heavy and large, so authority comes from scale and confidence rather than ornament — while Inter handles every reading and UI role and IBM Plex Mono is reserved for figures, keeping numbers tabular and unarguable.

The register is confident and expansive but never loud: 64–128px section rhythm, a 1280px container with a 680px reading measure, and full-bleed operational photography that is allowed to be the largest element on the page. This system is explicitly not a fintech dashboard, not a SaaS marketing template, not a marble-and-script luxury pastiche, and not an old-guard bank. Those four are confirmed anti-references, and every one of them fails the same way: they substitute a signal of credibility for the evidence of it.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Photography of real operations is proof, not decoration — it leads the viewport and the chrome recedes around it.
- Gold appears at most once per viewport section, as a rule or a single CTA, never as a large fill.
- Navy and sandstone alternate section by section to create editorial contrast without adding chrome.
- Display type is heavy and large; body type is quiet and long-measure. There is no middle register competing with either.
- Figures are always monospaced and tabular — a number should never reflow as it counts.
- Fully bilingual: English (Manrope + Inter) and Arabic (Cairo, RTL) are one coherent system, not a translated skin.

## Colors

A two-mode palette: Sandstone is the default reading world, Gulf Navy is the authority world, and Desert Gold is the only voice that crosses between them.

### Primary
- **Deep Gulf Navy** (`#071D49`): The authority surface. Hero, closing CTA, footer, and any section that needs to interrupt the reading rhythm. Also the primary button fill and the browser theme color. This is the color the brand is recognized by.
- **Gulf Navy Raised** (`#0D2657`): Cards and panels sitting on a navy section. Separated from its parent by tone plus a `border-ink` hairline, never by a shadow.
- **Gulf Navy Muted** (`#102B60`): The third navy step, for nested surfaces and subtle bands within a dark section.

### Secondary
- **Desert Gold** (`#B08D57`): The signature. Eyebrow hairline rules, the stroke beneath a stat figure, active navigation underlines, and the single gold CTA per section. On canvas it is never used for body-size text — it fails AA at reading sizes.
- **Desert Gold Soft** (`#D9C6A0`): The hover state of the gold button and the logo wordmark over dark photography, where full-strength gold would vibrate.
- **Desert Gold on Ink** (`#C9A96E`): The lifted variant used for gold text and eyebrows on navy surfaces, where `desert-gold` alone reads muddy.

### Tertiary
- **Charter Navy** (`#0F1B2D`) and **Charter Navy Tint** (`#1B2C45`): The near-black corporate navy used for the primary button's hover and active states, and for the occasional alternate dark section. Distinct from Gulf Navy: this one recedes, Gulf Navy asserts.

### Neutral
- **Sandstone** (`#F3F2ED`): The page background. A deepened, warmed off-white — deliberately not `#FFFFFF`, which reads cold and SaaS.
- **Sandstone Raised** (`#FBFAF7`): Cards, panels, form fields, and the scrolled navbar. The lighter surface that sits *on* the page.
- **Sandstone Muted** (`#E9E7DE`): Quiet section bands, neutral badges, and ghost-button hover fills.
- **Ink Text** (`#12151B`): Primary reading text on sandstone.
- **Slate Text** (`#5B6472`): Body secondary, descriptions, eyebrow labels on canvas.
- **Mist Text** (`#8E96A3`): Placeholders, captions, metadata, disabled states.
- **Parchment Text** (`#F7F6F2`) and **Muted Parchment** (`#9BA3B0`): The inverse pair on navy surfaces.
- **Border** (`#E4E2DA`) / **Border Strong** (`#CFCCC2`) / **Border Ink** (`#262B36`): Hairlines on canvas, input and outline-button strokes, and hairlines on navy respectively.

### Semantic
- **Success** (`#2F6E4E`), **Warning** (`#A9752B`), **Error** (`#B3403A`): Muted, desaturated, and warmed to sit inside the palette rather than shout out of it.
- **Focus** (`#3A5FCD`): A deliberately non-brand blue for focus rings, so focus is never confused with a gold accent or a navy surface.

### Named Rules

**The Rationed Gold Rule.** Desert Gold appears at most once per viewport section, and only as a hairline rule, an icon glyph, a small badge, an active-state underline, or a single primary CTA. Never gold-on-gold. Never a gold fill larger than a button. Never gold body text on sandstone. Its scarcity is the entire reason it reads as valuable.

**The No Pure Black or White Rule.** `#000000` and `#FFFFFF` do not exist in this system. Every neutral is warmed. A stark white card on a stark white page is the single fastest way to make this brand look like a template.

**The Alternating Worlds Rule.** Consecutive sections alternate between the sandstone world and the navy world. Two navy sections may never stack without a photographic or sandstone section between them — the contrast *is* the page rhythm, and stacking dark blocks collapses it into a single undifferentiated mass.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Manrope (with `-apple-system`, `Segoe UI`, sans-serif) — weights 500/600/700/800
**Body Font:** Inter (with `-apple-system`, `Segoe UI`, sans-serif)
**Data Font:** IBM Plex Mono (weights 500 and 700)
**Arabic:** Cairo, which takes over the display, body, and nav roles under `dir="rtl"`. Figures stay in Western numerals in both locales, so `--font-mono` is deliberately not remapped.

**Character:** A geometric grotesque set heavy and very large against a neutral, highly legible reading face. Manrope at 700 gives headlines the density of a printed annual-report cover without any of the fussiness of a serif; Inter disappears in long copy so the content is what registers. The mono figures are the third voice, and they only ever speak numbers.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, `clamp(2.75rem, 5vw + 1rem, 5.5rem)`, 1.02): Page-defining headlines. One per page, in the hero.
- **Headline** (700, `clamp(2.25rem, 3.5vw + 1rem, 4rem)`, 1.05): Sub-page and major section heroes.
- **Title** (700, `clamp(1.875rem, 2.5vw + 1rem, 2.75rem)`, 1.1): The default section headline — the most-used display size on the site.
- **Subtitle** (450, `clamp(1.5rem, 1.5vw + 1rem, 2rem)`, 1.15): The one light display weight, reserved for the home hero headline and large card titles where 700 would overpower the photography behind it.
- **Heading** (Inter, 600, 1.5rem / 1.125rem for small, 1.3–1.4): Component and card titles. Note these switch to the body face — display type stops at the section level.
- **Body Large** (400, 1.125rem, 1.6): Intro and lede paragraphs, constrained to the 680px measure.
- **Body** (400, 1rem, 1.65): Default reading text.
- **Body Small** (400, 0.875rem, 1.55): Navigation links, secondary copy, form labels.
- **Label / Eyebrow** (600, 0.875rem, `0.14em` tracking, uppercase): The section-opening label, always paired with a 32×1px gold rule beneath it.
- **Caption** (500, 0.75rem, 1.4): Metadata, disclaimers, badge text.
- **Data** (IBM Plex Mono 700, 3.75rem, 1.0, tabular): Stat counters. **Data Inline** (Plex Mono 500, 1.5rem) for figures inside cards and snapshot tiles. Both cuts are really loaded — never let the browser synthesize a bold at this size.

### Named Rules

**The Eyebrow-and-Rule Rule.** Every section opens with an uppercase, letter-spaced eyebrow above a 32px gold hairline, then the heading. This pairing is the system's most repeated device; a section that starts cold with a headline reads as an orphan.

**The Tabular Figures Rule.** Every number a visitor might compare — stats, returns, terms, dates — is IBM Plex Mono with `tabular-nums`. Counting animations must not cause a single pixel of horizontal reflow.

**The Two-Voice Rule.** Manrope speaks at section scale and above; Inter speaks at component scale and below. Card titles are Inter at 600, not small Manrope. There is no third register and no decorative face anywhere in the system.

## Layout

A centered 1280px container (`--container-max`) with responsive gutters stepping 16px → 24px → 32px → 40px → 48px → 64px from mobile to 2xl. Long-form copy is constrained to a 680px measure regardless of container width; a paragraph running the full 1280px is a defect.

Section vertical rhythm comes in three steps, each expanding at the `md` breakpoint: **small** 64→80px, **medium** 80→112px, **large** 96→128px. Large is reserved for hero and closing-CTA moments. Density is deliberately low — whitespace is the primary signal that this firm is not in a hurry.

The recurring structural device is the **split container**: a narrow 280px label column beside a flexible content column (`minmax(0,280px) 1fr`, 40px gap rising to 64px at `lg`), collapsing to a single stacked column below `lg`. Card grids run one column on mobile, two at `sm`, and three or four at `md`, with a 40px gap — generous enough that cards never read as a contiguous strip.

Full-bleed sections opt out of the container entirely (photographic heroes and the closing CTA) so imagery reaches both edges while their text content re-enters the 1280px grid via an inner wrapper.

**The Full-Bleed Photo Rule.** When a section carries a background photograph, the image goes edge to edge and the text sits inside the container over it — never a photograph inset in a rounded card with margins around it. Operations photography is a surface, not an illustration.

## Elevation & Depth

The system layers with visible lift. Depth is carried first by tone — sandstone → sandstone-raised → navy is a real z-order the eye reads before any shadow appears — and then reinforced by soft, wide, neutral shadows that make cards genuinely sit above the page rather than being drawn onto it. Shadows are always achromatic; a gold or navy-tinted shadow is never correct.

On navy surfaces the mechanism inverts completely: dark cards are separated by a 1px `border-ink` hairline, because a shadow against a dark background is invisible and only muddies the surface.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Resting** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(11, 14, 20, 0.04)`): Small chrome only — floating icon buttons, process markers, filter bars. Too slight to carry a card.
- **Lifted** (`box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(11, 14, 20, 0.06)`): **The resting state for every card on canvas.** This is where the system's visible lift lives; a card that sits flatter than this reads as printed onto the page rather than placed on it.
- **Floating** (`box-shadow: 0 16px 48px rgba(11, 14, 20, 0.10)`): Card hover, mega-menu panels, and modals.
- **Focus** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(58, 95, 205, 0.35)`): The focus ring on every interactive element. It is a shadow, not an outline, so it follows the border radius.

### Named Rules

**The Achromatic Shadow Rule.** Every shadow uses `rgba(11, 14, 20, …)`. Colored, glowing, or gold-tinted shadows are banned outright — they are the single clearest tell of the fintech aesthetic this system rejects.

**The Dark-Uses-Borders Rule.** On any navy surface, depth comes from a 1px `border-ink` hairline and a tonal step, never from a shadow.

## Shapes

Near-flat and rectilinear, at enterprise scale rather than app scale. Four radius steps do all the work: **4px** for inputs, checkboxes, and tags where a crisp edge signals precision; **8px** for buttons and small containers; **16px** for cards, feature panels, and modals; and **fully round** (999px) for pills and badges only. Nothing larger than 16px — a heavily rounded card immediately reads as consumer software.

Borders are hairlines, always 1px, and they are structural rather than decorative: they define a field's editable area, an outline button's target, or a dark card's boundary. The one intentionally decorative stroke in the entire system is the gold hairline rule, and it appears in exactly three places — under an eyebrow, beneath a stat figure, and as the active nav underline.

Photography is never masked into circles, arcs, or organic shapes. Rectangles, full-bleed or 16px-rounded, only.

**The Ghost Border Rule.** A gold border on a card or a button does not exist in this system. Gold is a rule, a glyph, a badge tint, or a fill on exactly one CTA — never an outline.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** Gently rounded (8px), never pill-shaped. Heights are fixed at three steps: 36px (`sm`), 44px (`md`, default), 56px (`lg`).
- **Primary:** Gulf navy fill with parchment text (`#071D49` / `#F7F6F2`), 24px horizontal padding at default size. The workhorse action.
- **Gold:** Desert gold fill with navy text (`#B08D57` / `#071D49`) — the single most important action on a page, typically "Request a Consultation." One per section, at most.
- **Hover / Focus:** 200ms `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`. Primary darkens to charter navy; gold lightens to desert-gold-soft. Focus shows the blue 3px ring. Disabled drops to 40% opacity with pointer events off.
- **Outline:** Transparent with a `border-strong` hairline; on hover the border goes navy and the fill goes sandstone-muted.
- **Ghost / Ghost-inverse:** No fill at rest; ghost hovers to sandstone-muted, ghost-inverse to `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` for use over photography.
- **Trailing arrow:** Primary CTAs carry a 16px `ArrowUpRight` that translates 2px up and right on parent hover. It mirrors under RTL. This is the standard CTA affordance and should not be replaced with a chevron or a right-arrow.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 16px on all four corners.
- **Background:** `sandstone-raised` on canvas sections; `gulf-navy-raised` on navy sections; `sandstone-muted` for the quiet borderless variant.
- **Shadow Strategy:** Lifted at rest, Floating on hover (see Elevation). Navy cards use a `border-ink` hairline and no shadow.
- **Border:** 1px `border` on canvas cards; transparent on muted cards; `border-strong` on the outline variant.
- **Internal Padding:** 24px / 32px / 40px, with 32px as the default. Card title is Inter 600 at 1.5rem; description is Inter 400 at 0.875rem in slate.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** 4px radius, `sandstone-raised` fill, 1px `border-strong` stroke, 48px height, 16px horizontal padding. Textareas add 12px vertical padding and resize vertically only.
- **Focus:** Border shifts to focus blue and the 3px focus ring appears; 150ms transition.
- **Hover:** Border lifts from `border-strong` to mist.
- **Error:** Border goes error red, with an `AlertCircle` icon and caption-size red message beneath, wired through `aria-describedby` and `role="alert"`.
- **Labels:** Always present, Inter 500 at 0.875rem, with a gold asterisk marking required fields. Placeholder text is never a substitute for a label.
- **Select:** Native element, appearance stripped, with a 16px chevron positioned 16px from the trailing edge.

### Badges
- **Style:** Fully rounded pill, 12px horizontal / 4px vertical padding, caption type at 500.
- **Variants:** neutral (sandstone-muted on slate), gold (12% gold tint with gold-on-ink text), navy (8% navy tint), on-ink (8% white on navy), outline, and success. Used for pool status, article categories, and sector tags.

### Navigation
- **Style:** Fixed, 80px tall, full-width. Over a hero it is transparent with a top-down black gradient scrim; past 8px of scroll it solidifies to `sandstone-raised` at 95% with a backdrop blur and a bottom hairline, transitioning over 300ms.
- **Typography:** Inter 500 at 0.875rem for links; the logo wordmark is Manrope 700 at 1.75rem with a 0.625rem uppercase legal-name line beneath it.
- **States:** Links are desert-gold-soft over the hero and desert-gold-on-ink once scrolled, brightening to parchment / gold-soft on hover.
- **Mega menu:** Hover-triggered on desktop with a 200ms close delay, opening as a multi-column panel with a heading per column and a description line per link. Mobile collapses to a full-screen sheet with height-animated accordion sections.

### Stat Counter (signature component)
The system's most distinctive element and the clearest expression of the North Star. A monospaced figure at 3.75rem with `tabular-nums` counts up from zero via a spring the first time it enters the viewport, then a 24×1px gold hairline, then the label in Inter 500, then an optional caption. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the spring is stiffened so the value lands effectively instantly rather than being disabled outright. Deployed in grids of two, three, or four with a 40px gap.

### Motion
Every component draws from one shared easing curve, `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, and three duration bands: 180ms micro-interactions, 500ms content reveals, 800ms section choreography with an 80ms stagger between children. Reveals are fade-plus-16-to-24px rise, triggered by scroll (`once: true`, `-80px` margin), never by load — the hero's single orchestrated entrance is the only exception. `prefers-reduced-motion` is honored globally in CSS and per-component through `useReducedMotion`.

**The Reveal-Don't-Bounce Rule.** No spring overshoot on entering content, no parallax, no decorative looping animation. The only permitted infinite animations are the skeleton shimmer and the marquee, both of which are functional.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** open every section with the eyebrow + 32px gold hairline + heading pattern.
- **Do** alternate sandstone and navy section by section to build page rhythm without adding chrome.
- **Do** use real photography of the operating businesses — cargo, cranes, sites, the Dubai skyline — full-bleed, with a gradient scrim carrying the text.
- **Do** set every comparable figure in IBM Plex Mono with `tabular-nums`.
- **Do** constrain long-form copy to the 680px measure even inside a 1280px container.
- **Do** rest every canvas card at `0 4px 16px` and lift it to `0 16px 48px` on hover; reserve `0 1px 2px` for small chrome.
- **Do** use `border-ink` hairlines instead of shadows on every navy surface.
- **Do** treat Arabic as a first-class locale: Cairo across every text role, full RTL mirroring, and directional icons flipped.
- **Do** keep the focus ring blue (`#3A5FCD`) so focus is never mistaken for brand accent.
- **Do** pull icons from `lucide-react` at stroke width 1.5, sized with the `size` prop.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use gold as a border, a large fill, a body-text color on sandstone, or more than once per viewport section.
- **Don't** ship `#000000` or `#FFFFFF` anywhere — every neutral in this system is warmed.
- **Don't** stack two navy sections without a sandstone or photographic section between them.
- **Don't** tint a shadow. Every shadow is `rgba(11, 14, 20, …)`; glows are banned.
- **Don't** exceed a 16px corner radius on cards, or make buttons pill-shaped.
- **Don't** set card titles in Manrope — display type stops at section scale, and headings below it are Inter 600.
- **Don't** add spring overshoot, parallax, floating blobs, gradient meshes, or any decorative looping animation.
- **Don't** mask photography into circles, arcs, or organic shapes, or inset it as a small illustration inside a card.
- **Don't** reach for stock imagery, clipart icons, "trusted by" logo walls, marble textures, or script fonts — the four confirmed anti-references are fintech/crypto, generic SaaS marketing, luxury cliché, and old-guard bank.
- **Don't** replace a label with a placeholder, or remove a focus outline without providing the ring.
